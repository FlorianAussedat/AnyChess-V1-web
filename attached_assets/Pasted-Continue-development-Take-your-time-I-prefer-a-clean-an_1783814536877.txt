Voice recognition
Keep the microphone as a toggle.
One tap enables continuous listening.
Another tap disables it.
Do not require pressing the button before every move.
Continuous listening
The microphone should stay listening continuously while the toggle is ON.
The only moment it should stop listening is while the app is speaking, to avoid interference with speech recognition.
After speech playback finishes, listening should automatically restart as quickly as possible.
Use the longest listening session possible. If the operating system forces the recognition session to restart periodically, keep the interruption as short as possible.
For now, do not handle screen locked / screen off mode. The phone screen will always stay ON during use.
Feedback
When a move is:
recognized,
understood,
legal,
and successfully played,
give:
a short vibration,
and a very soft confirmation sound.
When a move is:
illegal,
ambiguous,
or not understood,
give only:
a short soft buzzer,
then immediately continue listening.
Do not play any voice message in this situation.
Never confirm a move before it has actually been played.
